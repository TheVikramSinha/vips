Acceptance (Underwriting)

Insurance Domain #
Also known as: Accept
Definition

The underwriting decision indicating that a risk meets the insurer's appetite and may proceed to policy issuance without manual intervention or conditions beyond standard terms.

Use Case in Product Studio

Example
